Sorare reward thresholds by competition

This page lists how many SO5 points it took to finish in the essence places of each Sorare competition, measured across 26 completed gameweeks of the current season. Every number is a median over those gameweeks, so a single lucky or brutal week does not distort it. The page is rebuilt every night from the same data the live pages use.

  • Season coverage: 26 completed gameweeks, most recent starting 2026-10-27.
  • Competitions and rarities listed: 48.
  • Valuation accuracy is measured against real manager to manager sales and republished every night.
